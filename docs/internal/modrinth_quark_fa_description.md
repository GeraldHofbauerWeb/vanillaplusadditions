**Fresh Animations for Quark's own mobs.** Fresh Animations brings vanilla mobs to life, but it
only knows vanilla - Quark's creatures kept moving like stock Minecraft right next to them. This
pack fills that gap, in Fresh Animations' style.

> ⚠️ **The Toretoise only works with the [VanillaPlusAdditions](https://modrinth.com/mod/vanillaplusadditions) mod.**
> Quark walks the Toretoise's legs while drawing them, and that breaks - the game crashes - as soon
> as any resource pack gives it a new model. The fix is a few lines of code, so it can only ship
> in a mod. **This resource pack therefore covers every mob except the Toretoise.** The mod carries
> the complete set built in, Toretoise included, and switches it on by itself.

## Requirements

- [Entity Model Features](https://modrinth.com/mod/entity-model-features) (and
  [Entity Texture Features](https://modrinth.com/mod/entitytexturefeatures))
- [Fresh Animations](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/fresh-animations) - put **this pack above it**
- [Quark](https://modrinth.com/mod/quark), Minecraft 1.21.1

## What moves

| Mob | |
|---|---|
| **Foxhound** | Ears that twitch and fold back at a run, a sniffing snout, a tail that wags in bursts, and eyes with lids, a blink and a pupil that follows you. The legs no longer swing through the head. |
| **Crab** | Claws that open and close now and then, eye stalks that look around on their own, legs that shuffle while it stands. |
| **Stoneling** | Heavy, slow steps with the arms swinging along, and drowsy eyes: lids that hang half shut and a slow, heavy blink. |
| **Wraith** | The chain between its wrists is a solid 3D chain that swings, glowing pupils drift through the eye hollows, body and arms drift like a ghost's. |
| **Shiba** | Eyes with a blink and a gaze, and a real 3D tongue that slides out now and then, while sitting and while running - and pants. |
| **Toretoise** *(mod only)* | A heavy, rowing walk that lifts its feet in diagonal pairs, side eyes under heavy lids, and slow chewing. |
| **Forgotten** | Nothing to do: Quark draws it with the vanilla skeleton model, so Fresh Animations already covers it. |

Everything runs on top of Quark's own animation instead of replacing it - Quark still does the
walking, sitting and attacking, this pack adds the life around it.

## Resource pack or mod?

- **Resource pack only:** drop the zip into `resourcepacks`, enable it above Fresh Animations. All
  mobs except the Toretoise.
- **With VanillaPlusAdditions:** nothing to install here at all. The mod's `quark_fresh_animations`
  module adds the complete pack by itself whenever Quark and Fresh Animations are present. Should
  you put this zip into `resourcepacks` as well, the mod steps back so the two cannot cover each
  other - the Toretoise still comes from the mod.

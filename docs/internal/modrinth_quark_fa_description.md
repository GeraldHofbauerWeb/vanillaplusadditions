**Fresh Animations for Quark's mobs.** Fresh Animations stops at vanilla, so Quark's creatures
kept moving like stock Minecraft right next to the vanilla ones. This pack gives them the same
treatment, in Fresh Animations' style.

> ⚠️ **The Toretoise needs the [VanillaPlusAdditions](https://modrinth.com/mod/vanillaplusadditions) mod.**
> Quark moves the Toretoise's legs while it draws them, and the game crashes as soon as any
> resource pack gives it a new model. The fix is a few lines of code, which a resource pack cannot
> carry, so this pack covers every mob except the Toretoise. The mod has the complete set built in,
> Toretoise included, and switches it on by itself.

## Requirements

- [Entity Model Features](https://modrinth.com/mod/entity-model-features) (and
  [Entity Texture Features](https://modrinth.com/mod/entitytexturefeatures))
- [Fresh Animations](https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/fresh-animations), with **this pack above
  it** in the list
- [Quark](https://modrinth.com/mod/quark), Minecraft 1.21.1

## What moves

| Mob | |
|---|---|
| **Foxhound** | Ears that twitch and fold back at a run, a sniffing snout, a tail that wags in bursts, and eyes with lids, a blink and a pupil that follows you. The legs no longer swing through the head. |
| **Crab** | Claws that open and close now and then, eye stalks that look around on their own, legs that shuffle while it stands. |
| **Stoneling** | Heavy, slow steps with the arms swinging along, and drowsy eyes: lids that hang half shut and a slow, heavy blink. |
| **Wraith** | The chain between its wrists is a solid 3D chain that swings, glowing pupils drift through the eye hollows, and body and arms drift like a ghost's. |
| **Shiba** | Eyes that blink and look at you, and a real 3D tongue that slides out now and then, sitting or running, and pants. |
| **Toretoise** *(mod only)* | A heavy, rowing walk that lifts its feet in diagonal pairs, side eyes under heavy lids, and slow chewing. |
| **Forgotten** | Nothing to do: Quark draws it with the vanilla skeleton model, so Fresh Animations already covers it. |

Everything runs on top of Quark's own animation. Quark still does the walking, sitting and
attacking; this pack adds the rest: eyes, ears, tongues, the wraith's chain and the small idle
movements.

## Resource pack or mod?

- **Resource pack only:** drop the zip into `resourcepacks` and enable it above Fresh Animations.
  Every mob except the Toretoise.
- **With VanillaPlusAdditions:** nothing to install here. The mod's `quark_fresh_animations`
  module adds the complete pack whenever Quark and Fresh Animations are present. If this zip is in
  `resourcepacks` as well, the mod leaves its own copy out so the two cannot overlap; the Toretoise
  still comes from the mod.
